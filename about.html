About

Hello, I’m Sadia

Aidas Prints is me, Sadia, making things in Canberra. I print at home, knit by hand, and sell at local markets — where we can talk and you can hold the work.

How it started

I have always made things. Knitting came first — the quiet kind of craft you can do while talking. Then 3D printing arrived and opened a door: suddenly I could make almost any small object someone described to me.

Now I design pieces in Meshly and print them at home. A hook for a caravan, a cake topper with a grandchild’s name, a stand for a phone. If you can picture it, we can usually make it.

Why on demand

I do not keep a big stock. I make what people actually want, one at a time, in the colour they like. It is slower, and I think that is the point.

Why the markets

A stall is a real place. You can see the colours in daylight, feel the knitting, and ask me anything. Pay with a tap on my Square machine, or PayID if that is easier. I would rather meet you than send you a box in the post.

How to reach me

Calling or texting is the best way. Come to a market if you can. Tap Square at the stall, or PayID when we arrange it.
