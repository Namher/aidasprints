How a custom piece happens

Three easy steps. No jargon, no fuss.

  1. Tell me your idea

    Call, text, or email with what you have in mind. A photo or a sketch helps, but a chat is enough.

  2. I design and print it

    I design it at home on my computer, pick a colour with you, and print it.

  3. Meet me, or I post it

    We can meet if that suits you, or I can post the piece to you. Then you tap my Square machine, or pay by PayID.
