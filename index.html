How a custom piece happens

Three easy steps. No jargon, no fuss.

  1. Tell me your idea

    Call or text with what you have in mind. A photo or a sketch helps, but a chat is enough.

  2. I design and print it

    I shape the model in Meshly, pick a colour with you, and print it right here in Canberra.

  3. Pick it up

    Collect it at my next market or arrange a time in Canberra. Then it is yours.
